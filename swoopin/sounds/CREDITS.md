# Sound credits

Every recording here was cut to length, trimmed to its onset, peak-normalised,
faded, downmixed to mono and re-encoded as Ogg Vorbis. The two field recordings
from Wikimedia Commons were also high-passed, and the raven was de-noised.

Files from **CC BY-SA 4.0** sources are adaptations and are themselves
licensed CC BY-SA 4.0. **CC0** files are public domain. Thank you to all the
recordists.
